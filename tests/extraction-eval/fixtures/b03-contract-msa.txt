MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This agreement is made between:

  (1) Pellew Analytics Limited, a company registered in England, whose
      registered office is at 5 Cotham Hill, Bristol BS6 6LD
      ("the Supplier"), pellew-analytics.example; and

  (2) Northgate Dental Practice ("the Client").

1. TERM
   This agreement commences on 1 April 2026 and continues for twelve months,
   renewing annually unless either party gives ninety days' written notice.

2. CHARGES
   The Client shall pay the Supplier a fixed annual fee of 18,000.00 GBP,
   invoiced quarterly in advance.

3. CONFIDENTIALITY
   Each party shall keep confidential all information disclosed by the other
   which is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be so.

4. DATA PROTECTION
   The Supplier acts as a processor. The Supplier shall not transfer personal
   data outside the United Kingdom without the Client's prior written consent.

Signed for and on behalf of Pellew Analytics Limited
Signed for and on behalf of Northgate Dental Practice
